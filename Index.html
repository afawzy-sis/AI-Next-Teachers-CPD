From Idea to Design

Brainstorming & AI Presentation Decks

ChatGPT Google Gemini Claude AI Kimi AI Chat.z.ai Canva AI

Session Objective

Master prompt engineering and model selection across Claude AI, Kimi AI, Chat.z.ai, ChatGPT, and Gemini to generate comprehensive unit outlines and structured slide presentations. Auto-convert text outlines into visually captivating slide decks with Canva Magic Studio.

Hands-On Activity

Build custom presentation prompts using Kimi AI and Chat.z.ai for deep contextual structuring, synthesize lesson plans with Claude AI, and render presentation slides and printable student worksheets in Canva.

Deliverable Output

1 Hr 15 Mins

NotebookLM Mastery

Deep Curriculum Synthesis & Research

NotebookLM (Focused)

Session Objective

Harness Google's NotebookLM as a grounded AI research partner. Learn to synthesize complex textbooks, curriculum standards, and PDF documents into trusted study guides and audio discussions without hallucinations.

Hands-On Activity

Upload subject curriculum guides and PDF chapters into NotebookLM. Generate citation-backed study notes, custom Q&A keys, and auto-generate an AI Audio Overview podcast summarizing key unit topics.

Deliverable Output

An interactive Source Notebook containing verified unit briefing docs, student study guides, and an AI-generated Audio Overview podcast episode.

1 Hr 15 Mins

Brisk Teaching

Instant Differentiated Pedagogy

Brisk Teaching (Focused)

Session Objective

Streamline teacher workflow with Brisk Teaching chrome extension. Learn to instantly adjust reading Lexile levels, generate rubrics, create Google Forms quizzes, and provide targeted feedback directly inside Google Docs and web articles.

Hands-On Activity

Take any online article or complex lesson text, use Brisk to re-level it into 3 reading tiers (beginner, grade-level, advanced), auto-generate a 5-question comprehension quiz, and craft targeted student feedback templates.

Deliverable Output

A complete differentiated reading kit (3 Lexile levels) with an automated assessment quiz and grading rubric in Google Docs/Forms.

CapCut & AI Video Suite
